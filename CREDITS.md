All 41 model files are CC0. The included Three.js runtime is MIT, with its notice at `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

- **Forest (12 models):** Kay Lousberg, KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0 FREE. [Creator page](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest). Curated from the explicitly credited FREE-tier CC0 redistribution in [Station-Sciences/bot-crossing](https://github.com/Station-Sciences/bot-crossing), commit `72afc69ee2d42f2b6b88b41296f1ea9b23e03f83`. The official archive was not downloaded. Exact provenance, original credit and CC0 text: `licenses/forest/`. Per-model metadata: `forest-report.json`.
- **Kitchen (15 models):** TinyTreats Game Assets. Official [Charming Kitchen repository](https://github.com/TinyTreats-Game-Assets/Tiny-Treats-Charming-Kitchen-1.0) and [Baked Goods repository](https://github.com/TinyTreats-Game-Assets/Tiny-Treats-Baked-Goods-1.0). Original CC0 notices: `licenses/kitchen/`. Models repacked into self-contained GLBs with embedded atlases. Per-model metadata: `kitchen-report.json`.
- **Players (2 models):** Kenney, [Blocky Characters 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters), source characters B and E. External images were embedded; skeletons and 27 animation clips per character preserved. CC0 notice: `licenses/common/Kenney-Blocky-Characters-License.txt`.
- **Original common models (10):** Four gameplay tile modules, rolling pin, sugar cube, strawberry, meteor, mixer and daisy patch. Created for this pack and released under CC0: `licenses/common/Original-models-CC0.txt`.
- **Assembled scenes (2):** Original arrangements combining the above CC0 assets and independent tile nodes. CC0; original model credits retained here.

The models are stylized low-poly assets, not reconstructions of unrelated concept illustrations. No paid models, restricted-tier assets or paid generation API was used.
