The author page identifies the FREE tier as trees, bushes, grass and rocks, free for personal and commercial use under CC0. No EXTRA or SOURCE tier was downloaded.

It names the source directory as `assets-src/KayKit_Forest_Nature_Pack_1.0_FREE/Assets/gltf`, and lists all sixteen included models. Twelve are selected here. The mirror's original asset credits are retained as MIRROR-CREDITS.md; the software project's MIT license does not apply to these CC0 art files.

The source asset blob is `386f990dfc5580dd6c6063a1ee4838bdf4df6742`.
